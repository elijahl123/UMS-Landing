Calendar, Brightspace, and Canvas imports

Google Calendar sync can import events from a student-selected calendar, including “UCD Timetable.” Brightspace agenda PDFs and Canvas .ics calendar files are parsed locally in the browser, are not uploaded or retained by UMS, and require review before saving. UMS does not request Canvas credentials or a Canvas feed token. Imports and syncs may be incomplete or inaccurate. Students must review imported information and check official sources before relying on it.

Study plans for exams, assignments, and projects use predictable rules based on student-selected dates, estimated effort, available weekdays, and maximum daily time. If the available capacity is insufficient, UMS reports unscheduled work and requires acknowledgment before saving a partial plan. Study plans are not academic advice or AI-generated recommendations.
