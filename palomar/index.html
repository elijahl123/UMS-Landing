What AI-free means

No prompts. No AI credits. No productivity maze.

UMS is for students who want to organize schoolwork without AI. Other tools are your choice.

  • No AI chatbot and no LLMs.
  • No generated or rewritten student notes.
  • UMS does not send your schoolwork to generative-AI models or large-language-model services.
  • No academic recommendations you cannot inspect.
  • Study plans use dates, effort estimates, and availability you choose.
  • Every imported item and planned task can be inspected, edited, moved, or deleted.
